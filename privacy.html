Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the CPEN 321 M1 App ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is a student project created for CPEN 321 at the University of British Columbia and is intended for educational purposes only.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive the following from your Google account:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture
  • A unique Google account identifier

We may also store content you choose to enter in the App (for example, profile details such as a bio or hobbies). We do not access your Google contacts, files, calendar, or any other Google data.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and let you sign in
  • To display your profile within the App
  • To provide and improve the App's features

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing.

How your information is stored

Your information is stored in a database on a cloud-hosted server operated for this course project. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Sharing

Your information is only accessible to the project developer and, where required for grading, course staff. We may disclose information if required by law.

Google API Services

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data only as long as needed for the course project. You can delete your account from within the App, or email us to request deletion, and we will remove your data. All data may be deleted once the course ends.

You can also revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email adamjdl@student.ubc.ca.
